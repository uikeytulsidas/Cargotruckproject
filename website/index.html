In short: your game progress stays on your phone. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or contacts. Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

"Cargo Truck Simulator Driver" (the "Game") is published by Tulshidas Uikey, an individual developer in India (the "Developer", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play the Game and what choices you have.

The Game saves your progress on your phone only: cash balance, delivered routes and stars, trucks and paint you own, purchased items, and your settings (graphics, steering, sound). This file never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the Game.

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the Game asks for your consent to personalised ads before any are shown, and you can change that choice later from the Game's settings. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads. Buying "Remove ads" or the "VIP pack" turns off the automatic full-screen ads, and rewards are then given without a video.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details. The Game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed, so it can unlock the item. Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The Game does not require an account or login.
  • We do not sell personal data.

6. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game in a way that caused information to be collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and similar laws, you may have the right to access, correct or erase personal data, to withdraw consent, and to raise a grievance. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are handled by deleting your local save and adjusting your Google ad settings. For anything else, or to raise a grievance, use the contact details below; we aim to reply within 30 days.

8. Data retention and security

Local save data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the Game. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies. We use Google's standard, secure software libraries for ads and purchases.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, show a notice in the Game.
